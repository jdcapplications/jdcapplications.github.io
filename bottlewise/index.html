BottleWise

Wine, without the guesswork

Photograph the wine list. Know what to order.

A two-minute quiz tells BottleWise what you like. After that, snap a restaurant wine list or a store shelf and it picks the wines that fit you, each with a one-line reason in plain English. No jargon, and no guessing at names you don't recognize.

What it does

Scan a wine list

Photograph the list at a restaurant, or a menu or chalkboard. BottleWise reads every wine on it and gives you five picks: your two best matches, plus three “stretch picks” that are a little outside your usual taste but worth a try. Each one comes with a short reason.

Scan a store shelf

Standing in front of a wall of wine? Photograph the shelf in the same way. BottleWise picks the bottles that suit you and circles where each one sits in your photo, so you can find it on the shelf.

Scan a single bottle

Holding a bottle you don't know? Scan the label to see what it tastes like and how well it fits your palate, then save it to your wines.

Narrow it down

Only want a red tonight, a sparkling wine, or something under $30? Choose a wine type, a style or grape, and a budget before you scan, and the picks stay within them.

Learns as you drink

Rate the wines you try as Love, Like or Dislike, and your taste profile adjusts. A taste chart shows where you sit on sweetness, body, tannin, acidity and alcohol.

How it works

1

Tell us what you like

Name a few wines you enjoy, pick the flavors you like and the ones you don't. That builds your starting taste profile.

2

Point your camera

At a wine list, a shelf or a bottle, or choose a photo you already took. Results arrive in about 20 seconds.

3

Order, then rate it

Pick from your matches with a clear reason behind each one. Rate what you drank, and your next picks get more personal.
