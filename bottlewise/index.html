What it does

📷 Scan the wine list

Snap a photo of the list at any restaurant. BottleWise reads it, matches each wine against your taste profile, and ranks the ones you're most likely to love.

🍷 Scan a single bottle

Curious about a bottle in your hand? Scan the label for a plain-language read on its flavor profile and whether it fits what you like.

🏪 Scan the shelf

Facing a wall of wine at the store? Scan the shelf and BottleWise surfaces the bottles worth your money, ranked for your taste.

🍽 Built around your taste

A short flavor quiz builds your profile from the start, and every pick is explained in language that actually means something. Food pairing helps you match wine to the meal in front of you.

How it works

1

Tell us what you like

A quick flavor quiz sets up your taste profile. It sharpens the more you use the app.

2

Point your camera

At a list, a bottle, or a shelf. BottleWise reads it on the spot.

3

Order with confidence

Get ranked picks with a clear reason behind each one. Save the wines you love to come back to.
